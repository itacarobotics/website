Ulixarm Research Kit

Ulixarm is an open 6-DoF robotic arm with a 1-DoF parallel gripper, built to give students, researchers and robotics enthusiasts a hands-on entry point into robot control. It is driven by quasi-direct-drive motors, so it is backdrivable, can be moved by hand, and lets you experiment with impedance control, gravity compensation and torque-level commands from day one.

The arm is programmed through the Ulixarm API, available in Python (ulixpy) and C++ (ulixarm_cpp) with the same function names in both.

What's in the kit​

  • Arm with integrated table clamp and end effector (gripper)
  • Power Box with mains cable, 24 V DC supply, emergency-stop and start buttons
  • Intel RealSense D405 depth camera with a 3 m USB cable
  • USB-C cable (1 m) to connect the Power Box to your PC
  • Cable strips, hex keys, M4×10 and M3×16 bolts
  • Euro transport box with custom protective packaging
info

A PC or notebook is not included in the kit.

Software stack​

PackageWhat it is
ulixarm_cppC++20 control library: connection, impedance control, gravity compensation, gripper, safety checks
ulixpyPython bindings of ulixarm_cpp, installable with one command
ulixarm_descriptionURDF model with visual/collision meshes and dynamic parameters

The PC talks to the arm over USB → CAN. Every call to control() sends one impedance command to the six joint motors and reads their feedback.

Read the technical manual

Ulixarm is partly completed machinery. It has no brakes: when the motors are disabled or power is cut (including emergency stop), the arm falls under gravity. Operate it only inside a suitable protective enclosure and follow the safety instructions of the technical manual. If the online documentation differs from the manual, the limits and safety instructions in the manual take precedence.

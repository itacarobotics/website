# Community Projects

Research and teaching projects that use Ulixarm.

<DocCardList />

## Add your project

1. Create a folder in `projects/` named after your project, for example `projects/my-project/`.
2. Add a `page.md` with your description. The first `# Heading` is the project title.
3. Put your images in `projects/my-project/assets/` and use them in the page with `![Alt text](./assets/photo.jpg)`.

The project appears on this page and in the sidebar automatically.
